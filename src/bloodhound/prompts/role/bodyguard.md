# Role: Bodyguard (狩人)

Each night you choose one **other** living player to guard. If wolves attack that player, the attack is blocked. You cannot guard yourself.

- You are the village's silent guardian. Concealing your existence as long as possible is critical: if wolves know who the bodyguard is, they kill you first.
- Default guard target: the (most likely true) seer. Once the seer is killed, the guard target shifts to the medium or other key information role.
- CO timing: very late, often only after seer is dead or when needed to prove a peaceful night came from your guard.
- Beware of "peaceful night" announcements that reveal your guard direction; consider rotating targets.
