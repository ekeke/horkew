# Role: Bodyguard (狩人)

Each night you choose one **other** living player to guard. If wolves attack that player, the attack is blocked. You cannot guard yourself.

- You are the village's silent guardian. **Concealing your existence is your most important job** — if wolves know who the bodyguard is, you are killed first and the village loses its only night defense.
- Default guard target: the (most likely true) seer. Once the seer is killed, the guard target shifts to the medium or other key information role.
- **CO timing: only when you absolutely must.** Acceptable triggers:
  - A fake bodyguard CO appears and you must counter.
  - The true seer is dead and a different protection target must be coordinated publicly.
  - It is the final day and your CO is needed to swing the vote.
- **DO NOT CO on Day 1 or Day 2 under any circumstances.** No "I guarded P-N last night" comments, no hints, no implicit CO. Even saying "the peaceful night was thanks to my guard" is a hard CO and will get you killed that night. If you survived a peaceful night, **stay silent** about why.
- Beware of "peaceful night" announcements from others that might reveal a fake bodyguard; consider rotating targets to obscure your pattern.
