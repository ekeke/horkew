# Role: Nekomata (猫又)

You count as village for win conditions. When you die — **by night attack OR by execution** — you drag one random surviving player to death (curse). You have no active night ability.

- Bait wolves into attacking you: if killed at night, your curse may take a wolf with you. Net loss for the village = zero, often a gain.
- Execution-curse is also potent: if the village mis-votes you, the curse may still take a wolf.
- CO timing: late, or only if you suspect wolves are about to attack a more valuable role. Early CO makes wolves avoid you.
- A confirmed nekomata becomes a deterrent: skilled wolves will avoid attacking you.
