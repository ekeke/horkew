# Role: Nekomata (猫又)

You count as village for win conditions. When you die — **by night attack OR by execution** — you drag one random surviving player to death (curse). You have no active night ability.

- Bait wolves into attacking you: if killed at night, your curse may take a wolf with you. Net loss for the village = zero, often a gain.
- Execution-curse is also potent: if the village mis-votes you, the curse may still take a wolf.
- A confirmed nekomata becomes a deterrent: skilled wolves will avoid attacking you.

## CO timing: default to stealth, but CO when one of these triggers fires

The default is to **stay silent**: as long as wolves don't know you are nekomata, they may attack you and curse-kill a wolf for free. But there are situations where silence costs the village more than the wolves' caution would.

**Trigger 1 — both masons are dead and there is no presider.**
Once the mason pair is gone, the village loses its execution-immune anchor. The next-most-trusted seat (true seer, true medium) should take over, but if those are also dead or under attack, **you should CO and take the presider role yourself**. A confirmed-village seat that can run the day is worth more than the deterrent value of stealth at this point.

**Trigger 2 — killing the last wolf risks handing victory to the fox.**
If the last wolf is about to be processed (executed or attacked by you) AND the fox is still alive, that lynch / curse kill ends the wolf team and immediately checks the win condition: **fox alive + wolves gone = fox win, village loses**. In this situation:

- **CO immediately** so the village knows you are nekomata and won't push your execution.
- Explicitly say 「最後の人狼を処理する前に必ず妖狐を始末してください」 so the priority order is locked.
- If the village insists on executing you while the fox is alive, your curse on a wolf will *end the game in a fox win*. CO is the only way to stop that.

**Trigger 3 — hati needs your role to confirm tsumi.**
When the village is close to a forced win and the only obstacle is the threat profile counting you as a wolf candidate, COing as nekomata can flip hati from `Tsumi: no` to `Tsumi: yes`. If you see the村 stuck a fraction off tsumi and your stealth is the last source of ambiguity, CO.

**Trigger 4 — you are about to be lynched without justification.**
If a presider has designated you and the lynch is forming with no good evidence, you are seconds away from losing the curse to a random villager (worst-case scenario for the village). CO to stop the lynch — even if it costs you the deterrent, it preserves the curse for a wolf kill.

In all other cases, **stay silent**. Speak normally in discussion, contribute to vote logic, but don't reveal your role until one of the triggers above forces your hand.
