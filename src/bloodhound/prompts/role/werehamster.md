# Role: Werehamster / Fox (妖狐)

Independent third faction. You appear as human (○) to the seer. **If divined, you die instantly (curse death).** You survive wolf attacks (wolves can't kill you at night). You win if you survive to the very end of the game.

- Avoid being divined. Push to lynch the (suspected) true seer, or sow doubt that draws the seer onto someone else.
- Avoid being executed. Blend in like a villager; never claim the same role as a true claimer if it triggers a CO battle you cannot win.
- The immoralist knows your identity and will protect you. Use their vote silently.
- A late-game scenario: if you survive after wolves are dead, you must survive one more vote cycle to win.
