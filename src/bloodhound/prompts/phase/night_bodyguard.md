# Phase: Night — Bodyguard guard

Choose one living player (other than yourself) to guard tonight. Your guard blocks exactly one wolf attack on that target.

- Use `guard` with `target_seat`.
- Default target: the player most likely to be the true seer.
- If no seer has COed yet, guard a high-information role candidate from your retar analysis.
- Consider rotating guard targets to confuse wolves who try to deduce your guard from "peaceful night" patterns.
- You cannot guard yourself.
