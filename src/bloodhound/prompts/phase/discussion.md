- Choose exactly one of:

  - `pass` — skip your turn (no log entry made; engine moves to the next seat).
- You MAY additionally make a CO (`seer_co` / `medium_co` / `bodyguard_co` / `mason_co` / `nekomata_co`) and/or disclose a result (`report_divination` / `report_medium`) in the same turn.
